```
# Harbor View Tower — Project Glossary

## Harbor View Tower Overview
5-story mixed-use residential building. 312 units across 5 floors. General Contractor: Apex Construction Group. Owner: Harbor Development LLC. Built: 2024–2026. Located at 1200 Harbor Blvd, Oakland, CA.

## Element Types
Windows (prefix W): Fixed and operable window assemblies. Unit IDs: W-[floor][unit] format (e.g., W-301 = Window, 3rd floor, unit 01). 12 windows per floor in north wing (units 301-312). Window types: fixed, casement, awning, sliding.
Doors (prefix D): Interior and exterior door assemblies. Unit IDs: D-[floor][unit] format.
Walls: Load-bearing walls (concrete/CMU), non-load-bearing partitions (steel stud + gypsum board). Structural walls require engineer sign-off for modifications.
Floors: Concrete slab on grade (floors 1-2), post-tensioned slab (floors 3-5). Surface finishes: hardwood, tile, carpet.
Ceilings: Gypsum board suspended ceiling systems. Ceiling height: 9 ft standard, 12 ft lobby.

## Materials
Aluminum Frames (standard spec): Thermally broken aluminum window frames. U-value 0.30, SHGC 0.25. Supplier: AlumaCraft Inc. Lead time: 4 weeks for orders ≤10 units, 6 weeks for >10 units. Unit cost: $850.
Wood Frames (substitution-equivalent spec): FSC-certified wood window frames. U-value 0.28, SHGC 0.24. Meets or exceeds aluminum spec per ENERGY STAR requirements. Supplier: Premium Wood Co. Lead time: 4 weeks for orders ≤10 units, 6 weeks for >10 units. Unit cost: $1,100. Weight penalty: +15 lbs/unit — structural review required for quantities >8 units.
Vinyl Frames: PVC frames. U-value 0.32. Not approved for exterior use above 3rd floor — wind load rating insufficient. Cost: $650/unit.
Glass: Double-pane insulated glass units (IGU). Standard size: 36"x48". Tempered required for ground floor and any pane within 18" of floor.
Steel: Structural steel sections (W-shapes, HSS). Used for moment frames and lintels. Any modification requires structural PE stamp.
Concrete: 4000 psi normal-weight concrete. Core drilling requires structural review.
Gypsum Board: 5/8" Type X for fire-rated assemblies, 1/2" standard for non-rated partitions.

## Locations
Floor 1 (Ground): Lobby, retail spaces, mechanical room, parking entrance. Units 101-112 (north wing).
Floor 2: Residential units 201-212 (north wing), community room, gym.
Floor 3: Residential units 301-312 (north wing). Target floor for aluminum-to-wood window substitution demo scenario. Units W-301 through W-312 are the affected window assemblies.
Floor 4: Residential units 401-412 (north wing), roof terrace access.
Floor 5 (Top): Penthouse units 501-508, mechanical penthouse.
Wings: North Wing (units 01-12 per floor), South Wing (units 13-24), East Wing (commercial, floors 1-2), West Wing (parking structure).
Zones: Zone A = North Wing residential, Zone B = South Wing residential, Zone C = Commercial east, Zone D = Parking/mechanical west.

## Abbreviations
W = Window assembly. D = Door assembly. CO = Change Order. RFI = Request for Information. PM = Project Manager. PE = Professional Engineer (structural). AHJ = Authority Having Jurisdiction. IGU = Insulated Glass Unit. SHGC = Solar Heat Gain Coefficient. FSC = Forest Stewardship Council. CMU = Concrete Masonry Unit. GC = General Contractor. HSS = Hollow Structural Section. NIC = Not In Contract. VIF = Verify In Field.
```
